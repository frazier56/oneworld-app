Rental applicants may provide an identity document for a particular request. We handle the file, document category, linked request, submission/review information and decision to support that request and reduce fraud. These uploads are intended for the restricted rental workflow, not public profiles. The applicant, authorized host for the property and authorized platform personnel may need access for review, support or security. Do not upload unrelated documents. A host must use an identity document only for the lawful rental purpose and protect it from unauthorized use.

Passkeys use a public-key credential; the fingerprint or face used to unlock it remains with your device or credential provider. Payment providers process payment details; platform records include payment status and references. We use information to deliver requested services, administer agreements, communicate about them, prevent misuse, maintain records and meet legal duties. For activities subject to GDPR, relevant bases are contract, legal obligations, legitimate interests in operating and protecting the service, and consent where required. Terms acceptance alone is not unrelated marketing consent.

### Mobile app, device permissions and usage analytics

**Notifications.** If you allow notifications in the One World mobile app, we store a device token together with the platform, device name, app version and time zone, so we can send you notifications. They are delivered through Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and, on iPhone, Apple Push Notification service. You can turn notifications off at any time in your phone's settings.

**Camera and microphone.** The app uses the camera only when you choose to take a photo or video, and the microphone only when you record a voice note or dictate text. When you dictate, the audio clip is sent to Google's Gemini API to be turned into text and the text comes back to you; we do not keep the audio. Text you ask our writing assistant to draft or improve is processed the same way.

**Location.** With your permission, some features use your device's precise location: showing how far away events are, and sharing a place in Messages, which sends your coordinates as a map link that the people in that conversation can see. To turn your location into a city name, event features send the coordinates to a reverse-geocoding service. You can decline or revoke location permission in your phone or browser settings.

**Usage analytics.** We record our own usage events (for example, which screens are opened), with your IP address, an approximate city and country, your device and browser type and, when you are signed in, your account. We use them to run, secure and improve the service. We do not sell them. Usage events are not recorded when your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal.

Contact us about the providers and transfer safeguards applicable to your service. Where the law requires a safeguard for an international transfer, that requirement continues to apply.

We may disclose information when legally required, to address misuse or security issues, or in a business transfer subject to applicable law. Mobile numbers and SMS consent information are not sold, rented or shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes; delivery providers may process them to send authorized messages.

## 4. Choices, requests and retention

You can change available profile and communication settings, decline optional notifications or location permission, and request access, correction, deletion or a copy of your data through account controls or support. Depending on applicable law, you may also request portability, restriction, objection, information about automated evaluation, or review of a decision. We may need to verify your identity before disclosing or changing data. Do not email an identity document unless support has explained a suitable submission method.

Withdrawing consent stops the use relying on that consent going forward; it does not invalidate earlier lawful processing or remove another applicable legal basis. Contact support to withdraw consent or ask which records remain necessary. You may complain to your competent data-protection authority where applicable.

Retention depends on the purpose and record: operating an active account, completing a transaction, handling a dispute, proving consent and complying with legal duties may require different periods. Account deletion does not necessarily remove transaction, acceptance or legal-hold records immediately. We do not promise a fixed automatic deletion deadline for uploaded rental IDs; request removal or information about a particular file from support. We assess deletion requests against the continuing rental purpose and applicable retention obligations. Only provide the document needed for your request.

For Colombia, you may know, update and rectify your information, request proof of authorization and information about use, consult it without charge, and request revocation or deletion when legally available. Submit consultas or reclamos to the contact above, identifying your account and request. Applicable statutory procedures and time limits govern handling. For the EEA/UK, applicable access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability and objection rights and complaint rights remain available.

## 5. Children, security and updates

The platform is intended for adults aged 18 or older. Contact us if you believe a child's information has been submitted. Access controls and authenticated workflows help limit access; no website or transfer method can promise absolute security. Contact support promptly about suspected misuse.

Revisions identify their effective date. Material changes receive the notice and, where necessary, fresh consent required by law. Continued use is not treated as consent to a materially broader purpose that requires separate consent.
