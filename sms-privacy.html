SMS Privacy Policy

This policy explains how One World Labs, Inc. uses mobile numbers for text messaging across One World applications, including OneEvent.

Information we collect

We collect the mobile number a person provides, their SMS consent choice, consent timestamp, message delivery information, and replies such as STOP or HELP.

How we use mobile numbers

We use mobile numbers only for messages the recipient consented to receive, such as event invitations and reminders, application and ticket status updates, account notifications, and customer-support replies.

No sale or sharing for third-party marketing

We do not sell, rent, or share mobile numbers or SMS consent information with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. We may provide mobile numbers to service providers that deliver messages for us, solely to perform that service, or disclose information when legally required.

Message frequency and charges

Message frequency varies based on event activity, reminder choices, and account activity. Message and data rates may apply.

Your choices

Reply STOP to opt out of texts from that sender. Reply HELP for help. Consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of purchase.

Retention and security

We retain consent and messaging records only as long as reasonably needed to operate the service, document consent, meet legal obligations, and prevent abuse. We use reasonable safeguards to protect this information.

Contact

Questions about this SMS policy may be sent through One World customer support in the application.
